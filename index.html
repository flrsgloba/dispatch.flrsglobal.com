Pleasure is a practice.

The Pleasure Dispatch is an ongoing editorial publication by artist Freddie L. Rankin II, published through FLRSGLOBAL.

It follows pleasure as a daily pursuit rather than a finished state, moving between the studio, the table, the image, the object, the encounter, and the quiet details that make a life feel lived.

Each dispatch is part reflection, part record, and part invitation to consider pleasure without optimization.
