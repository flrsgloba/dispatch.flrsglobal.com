Pleasure is a practice.

The Pleasure Dispatch is an ongoing editorial project from FLRSGLOBAL, following pleasure as a daily pursuit rather than a finished state.

It moves between the studio, the table, the image, the object, the encounter, and the quiet details that make a life feel lived.
