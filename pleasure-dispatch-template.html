No. 001 · 2026-08-29 · Ritual as Pleasure

Notes on Rhythm, Rest, and Return

01 — A Reflection

I've been thinking about the difference between ritual and routine.

It started in Miami. I went down to document Kennedy, in the studio, at rest, and ended up paying attention to the small stuff: the pauses, the habits, the way her days had a shape to them. What makes something sustain you, versus something you just do.

I carried that question to Baltimore.

The residency let me settle into my own rhythms. Breakfast and coffee. Gym. Lunch. Shower. Studio. Dinner. Libation.

In the studio I kept circling the same words. Discipline. Strength. Sensuality. Shame. Balance. Worship. Pleasure.

Maybe ritual isn't about repeating something. Maybe it's about which things you keep returning to, because they give something back.

02 — The Work

Surface Tension

I keep thinking about what happens just beneath the surface — that space where something is being held, where there's pressure but nothing has quite given way yet. There's something sensual in the waiting, in not knowing when or if something will break.
