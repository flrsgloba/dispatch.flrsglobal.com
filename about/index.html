ABOUTThe Pleasure Dispatch

Pleasure is a practice.

The Pleasure Dispatch is an independent editorial publication by artist Freddie L. Rankin II, published through FLRSGLOBAL.

It is an ongoing exploration of pleasure without optimization: the ways pleasure appears in art, conversation, food, objects, travel, movement, ritual, rest, and the ordinary details of a life.

Each dispatch moves between the studio and the world around it. Some are reflections. Some are records. Some are invitations. Together they form an evolving archive of what it means to pay attention to pleasure.
